# Time & Productivity Estimation Rules

**Version:** 1.0 Draft  
**Last reviewed:** 12 November 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

Estimate project schedule and labor productivity.

## 2. Rules

### 2.1 Duration
- Per activity, duration is estimated by quantity × productivity rate.
- Productivity rate is a user-curated value (examples: m³/day per worker).

### 2.2 Labor
- Labor quantity = Σ(activities × number workers × days).

### 2.3 Dependencies
- Sequence of activities is defined by a drawing or user.

### 2.4 Overlap and Risks
- Risk is estimated per activity and is a user-curated value.
